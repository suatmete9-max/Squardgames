SquardGames - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Contact: suatmete9@gmail.com

What we collect

Player name and country you enter, a random Player ID, your game scores, approximate country from your IP address, and (if you contact us or claim a prize) the e-mail address and wallet address you type in.

Advertising

We show ads from Google AdMob (in the app) and other ad networks (on the website). These partners may use device identifiers such as the advertising ID and cookies to show and measure ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. Users in the EEA/UK are asked for consent.

How we use data

To run leaderboards, prevent cheating, answer support requests, and show ads. We do not sell your personal data.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices
