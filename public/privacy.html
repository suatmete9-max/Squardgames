Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information SquardGames ("we", "us") collects when you use our website, installable web app and Android app, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

  • Account details you enter: player name and country.
  • Generated data: a random Player ID and recovery key (we store only a hashed form of the key for verification).
  • Game data: your scores, game sessions and invites.
  • Approximate location: country estimated from your IP address (used to pre-select your country and to show country flags).
  • Contact and prize data: if you send a support message or claim a prize, the e-mail address, message and wallet address you type in.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a push token for your device.
  • On your device: your Player ID, language and settings are stored in your browser (local storage).

How we use information

To run the games and leaderboards, detect cheating, process prize claims, answer support requests, send notifications you asked for, keep the service secure, and show advertising. We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising and cookies

We show ads from third-party ad networks (Google AdMob in the app and other ad networks on the website). These partners may use cookies, device identifiers such as the advertising ID, and similar technologies to show and measure ads and may collect information such as your IP address and browsing data. You can control cookies in your browser and reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. Visitors from the EEA/UK are asked for consent where required. Please read the privacy policies of these ad providers for details.

Trending searches page

Our Trends pages show public information from Google Trends. Visiting them does not require an account.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the service (hosting, database, e-mail, advertising and notifications) or when the law requires it. The leaderboard shows your chosen name and country to other players; your e-mail and wallet are never shown publicly.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on secure cloud servers that may be located outside your country. We keep game and account data while your account is active and for as long as needed for prizes and fraud prevention. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

SquardGames is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or object to certain processing. E-mail suatmete9@gmail.com to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
